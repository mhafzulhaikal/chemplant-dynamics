ANSI/ISA-101.01 High Performance HMI Guidelines

Standardized Human Factors & Situational Awareness Engineering

Color Discipline in High Performance HMI
Color is reserved strictly for drawing operator attention to abnormal upset states. Static background elements must remain neutral dark gray/black tones.

1. Four Levels of HMI Displays

  • Level 1: Overview of entire plant / operating area.
  • Level 2: Unit Operation P&ID graphic display.
  • Level 3: Detailed equipment faceplates and diagnostic tuning modals.
  • Level 4: Sub-system diagnostics, interlocks, and maintenance procedures.

2. Visual Element Specifications

Element Standard Style Anti-Pattern (Prohibited)
Pipes & VesselsLow-contrast gray (`#2a3441`)3D gradients, skeuomorphic shading
Pumps & ValvesStatic outline icon with state badgeAnimated rotating impellers / flames
Alarm Warning (AH)Solid Amber badge (`#ffd600`)Flashing neon yellow text
