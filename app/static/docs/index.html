ChemPlant Dynamics — Technical Documentation

Real-time Process Dynamics, Control Engineering & High-Performance HMI Simulation Suite

Interactive Simulation Suite
ChemPlant Dynamics is an industrial-grade simulation and process control training platform adhering to ANSI/ISA-101.01 High Performance HMI principles and first-principles chemical engineering kinetics.

1. Available Simulation Modules

Stirred Tank Heater (STHR)

A continuous mixed heating tank modeling first-order thermal dynamics, liquid level gravity discharge, and non-linear valve hydraulics. Features dual SISO feedback loops (TIC-101 and LIC-101).

Explore STHR Documentation →

Biodiesel Transesterification Reactor

A continuous stirred tank reactor (CSTR) modeling Arrhenius reaction kinetics for triglyceride conversion with methanol, accompanied by exothermic energy balances and jacketed cooling control.

Explore Biodiesel Documentation →

2. Architecture & Standards

Every process model in this suite is governed by rigorous discrete numerical solvers (Euler / RK4) communicating with a zero-latency WebSocket bridge to the DCS faceplate layers.
